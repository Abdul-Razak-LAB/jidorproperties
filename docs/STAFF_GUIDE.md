# Staff guide

**Audience:** Jidor Properties sales, content, and administrative staff
**Status:** Intended workflow; available actions may differ until the requirements are implemented.

## 1. Staff access

- Use an individual staff account; do not share credentials.
- Use only the permissions needed for your role.
- Sign out on shared devices and report unexpected access or account activity.
- Password reset or access recovery should use the approved company process. Staff must never ask visitors to send passwords or verification codes.

The administration interface is not approved for production use until server-side sessions and authorization have been verified on every admin page and API.

## 2. Creating and publishing a property

1. Open the property management area and start a new draft.
2. Add an accurate title, property type, summary, full description, location, price/currency or approved contact-for-price label, and availability.
3. Add applicable dimensions, bedrooms/bathrooms, and amenities. Do not invent missing specifications.
4. Upload approved property images. Add useful image descriptions/alt text and order the images intentionally.
5. Preview the page on mobile and desktop. Confirm contact/consultation actions identify the correct property.
6. Publish only after required fields and commercial facts have been verified.
7. Update availability promptly when a property is reserved, sold, or otherwise no longer available.

Drafts should remain private. Avoid including private owner, resident, security, or access details in public descriptions or images.

## 3. Working a lead

1. Open the leads inbox and review new inquiries regularly.
2. Check request type, property attribution, preferred contact details, and submitted message.
3. Assign the lead to the responsible team member when assignment is available.
4. Contact the prospect through an approved channel and record the outcome using the approved status/follow-up workflow.
5. If notification delivery failed, use the authorized retry process and confirm the lead remains stored.
6. Escalate complaints, privacy requests, suspected fraud, or requests involving legal/financial commitments to the appropriate owner.

An email notification is not a substitute for the lead record. Do not export or forward lead details outside approved business systems.

## 4. Information handling

- Use personal information only to respond to the inquiry and for other purposes covered by approved consent and privacy notices.
- Do not download lead lists to personal devices or send them through unapproved messaging tools.
- Do not promise availability, returns, financing, title status, or legal outcomes that have not been verified and approved.
- Report incorrect listing data, suspicious submissions, and access issues promptly.
- Follow company retention and deletion instructions once approved.

## 5. Content quality checklist

- Correct spelling, location, currency, units, availability, and contact path.
- No placeholder claims, lorem ipsum, unsupported guarantees, or outdated prices.
- Photos represent the property, are authorized for use, and avoid identifying private information.
- Images have clear alt text when informative; decorative imagery is not redundantly described.
- The mobile detail page is readable, and all calls to action work.
